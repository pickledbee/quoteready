Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 (updated to add website measurement)

The short version

  • This website uses Google Tag Manager and related Google tools to count visits and see which pages are useful. It has no sign-up forms and no ads.
  • The live demo runs only in your browser. Nothing you type in it is sent or saved.
  • If you email us or buy from us, we use your information only to answer you, deliver what you bought, and keep normal business records.
  • We never sell or rent personal information.
  • When a business uses QuoteReady, its customer data stays in that business's own Google account. We do not receive it.

1. Who we are

TryQuoteReady.com and QuoteReady are offered by Bruce Bowers, doing business as HireBruce, in Wisconsin, USA ("HireBruce," "we," "us"). Questions about this policy: stradman100@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers (a) this website, TryQuoteReady.com, including the demo and the agency Client Launch Kit page, and (b) information we receive when you contact us, buy QuoteReady, or do a setup call with us.

It does not cover how a business that uses QuoteReady handles its own customers' information. If you asked a business for a quote through its QuoteReady page, that business is responsible for your information, and its own privacy policy applies. Contact that business directly.

3. Information we collect

When you visit this website

  • We use Google Tag Manager, a Google service that loads measurement tools such as Google Analytics. These tools may use cookies or similar technology to collect information like the pages you view, how long you stay, the link that brought you here, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or region). We use this only to understand how the site is used and improve it, not to identify you personally.
  • Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and the site will still work.
  • The site has no forms that send us data.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser. Anything you type in the demo stays on your device and disappears when you close the page.
  • The Client Launch Kit page builds links and code inside your browser. Nothing you type there is sent to us.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write.

When you buy

  • Your name, email address, business name, the plan you bought, and the Google account email you want us to share the QuoteReady template with.
  • Payments are handled by our payment processor (for example Stripe, Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad). We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount and limited details such as the last four digits of a card. We never see or store full card numbers.

During a Done for You setup call

You share your screen and stay signed in to your own accounts. We may see your screen, prices, logo and business details during the call. We do not ask for, type or keep your passwords, and we do not record calls unless you agree first.

4. How we use information

  • To answer your questions.
  • To deliver what you bought: share the template, send documents and license, and provide the support included with your plan.
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records.
  • To send you important notices about your purchase or license, such as an agency renewal reminder.
  • To send occasional product news only if you ask for it. You can stop these any time by replying "unsubscribe."
  • To protect our rights and prevent fraud or abuse.

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the business, such as our payment processor, email provider (Google), website host (GitHub) and website measurement provider (Google), only as needed for them to do that work.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • If the business is sold or transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

6. Your QuoteReady data stays yours

QuoteReady runs inside the buyer's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). Quote requests, customer names, emails and phone numbers that a business collects through QuoteReady go straight to that business's own Google account. HireBruce does not run a server that receives them and has no access to them unless the business separately chooses to share its sheet with us.

7. How long we keep information

We keep purchase and tax records as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years. We keep emails as long as needed to support you, then delete or archive them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see section 9). We may keep what the law requires us to keep.

8. Security

We use reputable providers with strong security, keep our accounts protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, and limit what we collect. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email stradman100@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's or country's law. We will honor them as required.

10. Children

This website and QuoteReady are for businesses. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
