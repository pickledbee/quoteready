Questions

Straight answers

Will this get me more customers?

We cannot promise that, and you should be careful of anyone who does. QuoteReady removes two common leaks: customers who leave because they cannot see a price, and requests that sit unanswered. Whether you win the job still depends on your price, your reviews and how fast you call back.

Is there really no monthly fee?

No monthly fee from us. It runs on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script in your own Google account, which is free for normal small-business volume, and on free GitHub Pages hosting. You only pay for a custom domain if you want one.

How many quote requests can it handle?

Plenty for most small businesses. The real limit is email: a free Gmail account can send to about 100 recipients a day through Apps Script (a paid Google Workspace account about 1,500). Each request uses 2 (your alert and the customer's copy). If you hit the limit, the lead is still saved in your sheet; only the email waits.

Who can see my customers' information?

Only people you share your Google Sheet with. The website never receives your lead list or your private settings. Never "publish to the web" or share the Leads tab publicly.

Can I change my prices?

Yes. Edit the Services, Options and Extras tabs. The website picks up the change within about 2 minutes.

Does it book appointments or take payments?

Not by itself. You can add your existing booking link (Calendly, Google Calendar booking pages, Square and others) and a "Book a time" button appears after the price. That keeps QuoteReady simple and cheap.

Does it send text messages?

No. Business texting in the U.S. requires carrier registration and paid services. Alerts come by email, which shows up instantly on your phone. The lead email has a one-tap "Text" button so you can text the customer from your own phone.

What if my business is not one of the six industries?

Start from the closest one and rename the services and questions. Any business that prices by size, count, or a few choices can use it: carpet cleaning, gutter cleaning, pet grooming, mobile detailing, moving help, photography packages and more.

Do I own it?

You own your copy, your data and your domain. The software is licensed to you for your business (agencies: for your clients). You can change it however you like for your own use. You cannot resell the code itself as a competing product.

What if I need help later?

Your plan includes email help with installation. After that, the guides and a troubleshooting checklist cover the common problems, and paid help is available.
